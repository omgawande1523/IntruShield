02 — The problem

Attacks hide inside normal traffic.

  • A single campus network sees millions of connections a day.
  • Attackers change tactics constantly. Yesterday's signature misses today's attack.
  • Security teams drown in false alarms and stop looking.

03 — Introducing

Introducing IntruShield.

One model, trained on real attack traffic, sits in front of the network and judges every connection before it does damage. The red blocks you see leaving the cluster are attacks being caught.

04 — The purpose

A shield that learns.

Protects

Campuses & small businesses

The teams that need protection most and can least afford a 24/7 security staff.

Protects

ISPs & SOC teams

A fast first filter that hands analysts a short, explained list instead of a flood.

Stops

DoS floods & network scans

Floods that knock servers offline, and the scans that come before them.

Stops

Break-ins & takeovers

Password guessing from outside, and insiders quietly escalating to admin control.

05 — How it works

Watch. Judge. Explain.

  1. 01

    Watch

    Reads 41 signals of every connection: how much data moved, how fast, to which service, how many handshakes failed.

  2. 02

    Judge

    Decides in microseconds whether it is normal or an attack, and which kind: flood, scan, break-in or takeover.

  3. 03

    Explain

    Shows the top reasons behind every verdict in plain English, so analysts can trust it and act fast.
